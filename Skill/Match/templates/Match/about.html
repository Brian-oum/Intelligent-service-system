About Fixkona

We're building the fastest way to find trusted help at home.

Our story

Home repairs shouldn't start with a guessing game

Finding a reliable tradesperson in Kenya shouldn't take three days of cold calls, Facebook groups, and hoping the person on the other end is actually qualified. Yet for most homeowners, that's exactly what happens — phone numbers that lead nowhere, no way to verify licensing or past work, and no accountability if the job goes wrong.

The result is a market built on guesswork: homeowners overpay for underqualified work, good tradespeople stay invisible because they have no way to prove their track record, and every job starts from zero trust. Fixkona exists to close that gap — describe the job in your own words, get matched with licensed, reviewed professionals near you, and book with confidence.

We're still a small team, but we're obsessive about the same thing every day: making it faster and safer to get quality work done on your home.
